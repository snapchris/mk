About me

I've been modeling for eight years, starting with runway in New York before moving into swimwear, lingerie and editorial work. You've seen me in campaigns for independent labels and on the pages of a few magazines I still can't believe I made.

Off set, I'm usually at the beach, in a dance class, or planning the next trip. I love collaborating with photographers and brands who have a clear vision and aren't afraid of a little drama.
